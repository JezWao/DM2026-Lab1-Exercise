# Your report

The Reddit stock sentiment dataset, Reddit-stock-sentiment.csv, was loaded using load_dataset_tool (load_dataset_1). It contains 847 documents with three sentiment categories: negative (-1.0), neutral (0.0), and positive (1.0). Data inspection (inspect_data_2) confirmed the dataset structure, while missing-value checking (check_missing_3) found no missing or empty text entries. Duplicate checking (check_duplicates_4) identified 23 duplicate rows, which were not removed. Descriptive analysis (describe_data_5) showed that document lengths vary considerably, with an average of approximately 149 characters.

Next, the documents were tokenized (tokenize_6) and converted into a document-term matrix (build_dtm_7) containing 847 documents and 4,346 terms. The matrix had 17,193 non-zero entries and approximately 99.53% sparsity, indicating that most documents contain only a small portion of the vocabulary. Term-frequency analysis (term_frequency_8) identified common words such as "the", "to", and "and". The heatmap (dtm_heatmap_9) visualized the first 20 documents and 20 terms. Variance filtering (variance_filter_10) reduced the vocabulary from 4,346 to 506 retained terms using a threshold of 0.01.

Pearson correlation filtering (pearson_filter_11, pearson_filter_12, and pearson_filter_13) and Spearman correlation filtering (spearman_filter_14, spearman_filter_15, and spearman_filter_16) were performed for all three sentiment categories. The results showed different term associations across sentiment classes. For negative sentiment, terms such as "he", "will", and "trump" had relatively strong positive correlations. For positive sentiment, terms such as "technology", "huge", and "nice" were among the strongest. Neutral sentiment was associated with terms such as "com", "stockbuyvest", and "updated". Pattern mining was skipped in this experiment.

Finally, dimensionality reduction was performed using PCA (reduction_17), reducing the original 847 × 4,346 matrix to 847 × 2. The first two principal components explained approximately 31.99% of the total variance. The resulting scatter plot, Jerrye_Eng_Zhe_homework_plot_reduction_17.png, provides a two-dimensional visualization of the documents. Label binarization (binarized_labels_18) also converted the sentiment categories into a three-column one-hot encoded matrix with 847 rows. However, its reported negative and neutral class counts were reversed compared with the original dataset distribution, so this discrepancy should be checked before using the encoded labels in further analysis.
